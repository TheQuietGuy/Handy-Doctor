How HandyDoctor works

Every response is generated fresh from verified clinical sources — not from a static FAQ or a hallucinating chatbot.

1
✍️
Describe your symptoms
Type in plain English. No medical jargon needed — just describe what you're feeling and for how long.
2
🔍
RAG pipeline retrieves
Your query is semantically matched against indexed NHS, WHO, and NICE guideline documents.
3
🧠
AI generates response
GPT-4 synthesises the retrieved context into a clear, cited answer grounded only in the retrieved sources.
4
🏷
Receive triage guidance
Every response includes a severity badge — Self-care, See GP, or Call 999 — and links to the source guidelines.
