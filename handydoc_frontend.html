Built to err on the side of caution.

Fixed red-flag rules based on NHS guidance run before the AI. The AI can make the advice more urgent, but never less urgent than those rules. Emergency and crisis messages get a fixed, safe response, and answers come from published clinical guidance, not the open web.

Questions

Is HandyDoctor a replacement for a doctor?

No. It helps you decide how urgently to get care and what to tell the clinician. It can't examine you, and it doesn't diagnose.

Where do the answers come from?

From a library of NHS, NICE and WHO pages that we index and keep up to date. Every answer links to the pages it used.

What happens if I describe an emergency?

Emergency and crisis wording is detected before the AI runs. You'll see an urgent response with the right number to call straight away.

Who can see my conversations?

Only you. You can download all your data or delete your account at any time from Profile & privacy.

Does it work for children?

You can ask about a child's symptoms, and answers will flag where children need to be seen sooner. For babies under 3 months with a fever, always call 111 or your GP.

Not sure if it's serious?

Ask HandyDoctor. It's free, and an answer usually takes seconds.
